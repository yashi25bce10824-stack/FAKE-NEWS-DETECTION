 📌 Problem Statement

## 📰 Fake News Detection Using Machine Learning

In today’s digital world, information spreads extremely fast through social media platforms, news websites, and messaging applications. While this makes access to information easier, it also increases the risk of spreading fake or misleading news.

Fake news can create confusion among people, influence opinions, and sometimes even lead to serious social consequences. Verifying every piece of news manually is not practical for users due to the large amount of content available online.

This project focuses on developing a machine learning-based system that can automatically detect whether a news article is real or fake. By analyzing the textual content of news using Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques and classification algorithms, the system provides a simple and efficient way to identify misinformation.

The goal of this project is to build a reliable and user-friendly solution that helps users make informed decisions and reduces the impact of fake news in society.
